# How to connect your HubSpot CRM

Polaris syncs your HubSpot contacts, deals, and pipeline data to unify it with your marketing metrics. Here's how to set it up.

## Before you start

- You must have **Admin** role in your Polaris workspace to connect or reconnect integrations.
- You'll need HubSpot login credentials and permission to authorize OAuth access.

## Steps

1. **Open Connectors**  
   From your workspace, go to **Settings > Connectors**.

2. **Find HubSpot**  
   Scroll to **CRM integrations** and click **HubSpot**.

3. **Authorize Polaris**  
   Click **Connect**. You'll be taken to HubSpot's login page.

4. **Sign in to HubSpot**  
   Enter your HubSpot credentials and review the permissions Polaris is requesting (read access to contacts, deals, and company data).

5. **Approve the connection**  
   Click **Authorize**. You'll return to Polaris.

6. **Confirm setup**  
   Your HubSpot account is now connected. Polaris will begin syncing data on the next scheduled sync (typically within 1 hour).

## What happens next

Data from HubSpot appears in your dashboards and is available for attribution and blending with your ad and analytics data. Syncs run automatically on your workspace's schedule (hourly or daily, depending on your plan).

## Troubleshooting

**Connection failed?**  
Ensure you have Admin access in both HubSpot and Polaris. If your OAuth token expired, reconnect using the same steps above.

**Data not appearing?**  
Check that the sync has completed (see **Connectors > Sync status**). If data is still missing after 2 hours, contact support.
