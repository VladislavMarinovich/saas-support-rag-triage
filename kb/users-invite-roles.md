# Inviting teammates and understanding roles

Polaris uses **roles** to control what your team can see and do within your workspace. Here's how to invite people and assign the right level of access.

## Invite a teammate

1. Go to **Workspace settings** → **Users & access**.
2. Click **Invite user**.
3. Enter their email address.
4. Select a role (see below).
5. Click **Send invite**. They'll receive an email with a signup link.

## Understanding roles

| Role | Permissions | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access; can invite/remove users; reconnect connectors (Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot, etc.); manage workspace settings | Team leads, workspace owners |
| **Analyst** | View all dashboards, metrics, and reports; create alerts and exports; cannot invite users or reconnect connectors | Marketing analysts, revenue ops |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to dashboards and reports; cannot edit alerts or connectors | Executives, stakeholders |

## Key points

- Only **Admins** can reconnect data sources (e.g., if GA4 auth expires). Plan ahead if your only Admin is unavailable.
- Roles are **workspace-specific**. Users in multiple workspaces can have different roles in each.
- You can change a user's role anytime in **Users & access**.
