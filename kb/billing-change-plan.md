# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

Polaris plans scale with your needs. You can change your plan at any time from your workspace billing settings.

## Prerequisites

- You must have **Admin** role in your workspace to change the plan.
- Plan changes take effect immediately.

## Steps

1. **Sign in** to Polaris and open your workspace.

2. **Go to Settings** → **Billing**.

3. **Review your current plan** (Starter, Growth, or Enterprise). Each plan shows:
   - Number of connectors (GA4, Google Ads, HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, Brevo, etc.)
   - Seat count
   - Data retention window
   - Features (alerts, attribution, scheduled reports, SSO on Enterprise)

4. **Click "Change Plan"** next to the plan you want.

5. **Confirm the change**. You'll see:
   - New pricing (pro-rated if mid-cycle)
   - Seat limit changes
   - Any feature differences

6. **Complete payment** (for upgrades).

## Downgrading

When downgrading, you may lose access to:
- Extra connectors (if you exceed the new plan's limit)
- Scheduled reports or advanced features
- Extended data retention

Review the plan comparison before confirming.

## What happens after

Your workspace updates immediately. Syncs (from GA4, Google Ads, CRM) continue on your existing schedule. Analysts and Viewers see no change.
