# Plans and what each one includes

Polaris offers three plans designed to scale with your marketing and revenue data needs. Here's what's included at each level.

## Starter
Best for small teams getting started with unified analytics.

- Up to a limited number of connectors (Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Brevo, and more)
- Smaller seat count — invite team members with Admin, Analyst, or Viewer roles
- Shorter data retention window
- Core features: dashboards, alerts, north-star metric tracker, and basic reports
- Standard support

## Growth
Ideal for scaling teams needing more integrations and deeper historical data.

- Expanded connector limits — connect more Google Ads accounts, GA4 properties, CRM platforms, and email tools
- More seats for your workspace
- Extended data retention — keep historical data longer for trend analysis
- All Starter features plus advanced attribution and scheduled exports
- Standard support

## Enterprise
For large organizations with unlimited integration and compliance needs.

- Unlimited connectors — integrate as many sources as you need
- Unlimited seats
- Full historical data retention
- Single Sign-On (SSO) for seamless workspace access control
- All Growth features plus priority support
- Dedicated success resources

## How to check or upgrade your plan

1. Log in to your Polaris workspace
2. Click **Settings** → **Billing**
3. Your current plan appears at the top
4. To upgrade or change plans, click **Manage Plan** and follow the prompts
