# Deleting your workspace or account: a staff-only request

Deleting a workspace, closing an account, or requesting erasure of your data cannot be done from Settings. These requests are verified and executed by Polaris staff, because they are irreversible and because we confirm ownership first. The assistant returns AG004 for them rather than attempting to help.

## Which deletion requests need staff, and what to send

Contact support directly for:

- **Deleting an entire workspace** and everything in it
- **Closing your account** and ending billing
- **Erasing personal data** held by Polaris, including data-protection requests
- **Removing a workspace created by mistake** or during a trial
- **Transferring ownership** when the only Admin has left the company

Include the workspace name, the account email, and whether you need exports first. Staff will verify that the requester is entitled to make the request before anything is deleted — expect an identity check, not immediate execution.

## What to do before requesting deletion

Deletion removes synced history along with dashboards, reports, and alerts, and it cannot be undone:

1. **Export what you need** from each dashboard's **Export** button (CSV, PDF, or Excel).
2. **Download recent reports** from **Reports** while they are still available.
3. **Disconnect connectors** in **Settings > Connectors** if you want syncing to stop immediately, before the deletion is processed.
4. **Cancel or downgrade the plan** in **Settings > Billing** if billing is the urgent part — that you can do yourself, straight away.

Deleting a single **dashboard** is different and fully self-service from the dashboard menu. Only workspace and account deletion require staff.
