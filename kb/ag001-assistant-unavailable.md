# AG001 — Assistant unavailable

Polaris shows AG001 when the support assistant cannot answer because the service behind it is not responding.

## What AG001 means for the rest of Polaris

AG001 is limited to the assistant. Dashboards, connectors, alerts, and scheduled reports are unaffected — they do not route through it. So a workspace can show AG001 while every sync and report runs normally.

AG001 is a temporary service state, not a permission or plan restriction: the assistant is available on every plan.

## What to do while AG001 is showing

1. Retry in a few minutes — most AG001 cases clear on their own.
2. Meanwhile, search the knowledge base directly; every answer the assistant gives comes from articles you can read yourself.
3. If you need help urgently, contact support through the normal channel rather than waiting for the assistant.

If AG001 lasts more than an hour, contact support with your workspace name and the time you first saw it.
