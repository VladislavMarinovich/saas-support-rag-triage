# PF004 — Seat limit reached

Polaris shows PF004 when you invite someone but your workspace already uses every seat your plan includes.

## What PF004 means for pending invitations

The invitation is not sent while PF004 is open, so the person never receives an email. Existing users keep working normally — PF004 only blocks adding one more.

Pending invitations that were already sent count against your seats, which surprises people: a workspace can hit PF004 with several invites still unaccepted.

## How to free a seat or raise the limit after PF004

1. Open **Settings > Users** and review the list, including invitations still pending.
2. Remove people who no longer need access, or cancel stale invitations — the seat frees immediately.
3. If everyone listed is active, ask an **Admin** to move the workspace to a plan with more seats in **Settings > Billing**. Starter has the fewest seats, Growth more, and Enterprise is unlimited.
4. Send the invitation again.

If **Settings > Users** shows fewer people than your plan allows and PF004 still appears, contact support with your workspace name.
