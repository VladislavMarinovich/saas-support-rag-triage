# Slack notifications for alerts

Slack delivery for alerts is **on our roadmap** and planned for an upcoming release. Today, alerts are delivered by email only.

## What's available today

- Alerts fire based on the thresholds you configure (see **How to create alerts**).
- Delivery channel: **email only** (to the workspace users you select as recipients).
- Frequency: real-time, as soon as the alert condition is met.

## Why not Slack yet

We're building Slack delivery as part of the next iteration of alerts. It will support:

- Sending alerts to a specific channel or DM
- Per-alert channel selection (different alerts to different teams)
- Rich formatting with the metric and context

## In the meantime

- **Email + filters**: Many teams route Polaris alert emails to a Slack channel using Gmail/Outlook filters + Slack's email-to-channel integration. It's not instant like a native integration, but it works today.
- **Watch this space**: When Slack support ships, existing alerts will remain unchanged; you'll just get a new "delivery channel" option in the alert settings.
