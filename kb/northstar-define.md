# Defining your north-star metric

Your north-star metric is the single KPI that matters most to your business—the one number you check first each morning to measure progress toward your goals.

## Steps

1. **Decide what you're measuring.**  
   Choose a metric from your connected data sources (Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot, email platforms, or CRM). Common examples: monthly recurring revenue (MRR), qualified leads generated, or customer acquisition cost (CAC).

2. **Go to North-Star Metric in your workspace.**  
   You'll need Admin or Analyst permissions to create or edit your north-star.

3. **Select your data source and metric.**  
   Pick the connector (e.g., "GA4") and the specific field (e.g., "Conversions"). Polaris syncs this data on your scheduled interval (hourly or daily, depending on your plan).

4. **Set a target (optional).**  
   Add a goal value so Polaris can show you progress at a glance.

5. **Save.**  
   Your north-star now appears on your workspace dashboard. All team members can see it, even Viewers.

## Important

- Your north-star pulls live data from your connectors. If a source hasn't synced recently, the number may lag.
- Only Admins and Analysts can change the metric; Viewers can only view it.
- Ensure your source data is complete and accurate—missing UTMs or unmapped fields will skew results.
