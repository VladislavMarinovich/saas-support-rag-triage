# AG004 — Answer withheld, needs a human

Polaris returns AG004 when your request belongs to a category that only Polaris staff can act on, so the assistant routes you to a person instead of answering.

## Which requests are always handled by staff

AG004 is not a failure of the assistant — it is the correct outcome for requests where an automated answer could cause harm or cannot be executed at all:

- **Billing disputes, refunds, and chargebacks** — a charge you believe is wrong needs a billing review by staff
- **Deleting a workspace or an account, and data deletion requests** — irreversible, so a person verifies ownership first
- **Suspected security incidents or unauthorized access** — routed to the security team immediately
- **Contract, invoicing, and procurement questions** — handled by our team, not documented in the knowledge base
- **Anything requiring a change to your account that you cannot make in Settings**

Note the difference from AG002: there the knowledge base has no article; here the request is deliberately reserved for staff.

## What happens after AG004

1. The assistant tells you the request is being routed to staff and stops there — it does not attempt a partial answer.
2. Contact support with the details of your case: workspace name, and the invoice, charge, or account in question.
3. For a suspected security issue, say so explicitly so it is prioritized; Enterprise workspaces receive priority incident response.
4. Expect a reply from a person rather than from the assistant.

If you received AG004 for a straightforward how-to question, rephrase it as a documentation question and ask again.
