# Billing disputes and refunds: when staff reviews your case

Some billing questions cannot be resolved in the product. A charge you believe is wrong, a refund request, or a chargeback is reviewed by a member of our billing staff — the assistant will not attempt to answer these, and returns AG004 instead.

## Which billing requests need a person

Contact support and ask for a billing review when:

- **You believe a charge is incorrect** — wrong amount, duplicate charge, or a charge after cancellation
- **You want a refund**, in full or pro-rated
- **You have opened or are considering a chargeback** with your bank
- **You need an invoice changed** — company name, VAT or tax ID, purchase order number
- **You are negotiating contract terms, procurement, or annual billing**
- **You cancelled and were charged anyway**

Include your workspace name and the invoice or charge in question. Staff can see the billing record; the assistant cannot.

## Which billing tasks you can do yourself

These do not need staff and are faster on your own, in **Settings > Billing** as an **Admin**:

- Changing plan up or down — takes effect immediately, pro-rated mid-cycle
- Updating the card or payment method, including after a declined payment (PL003)
- Retrying a failed payment
- Checking which plan you are on and what it includes

Note the distinction: a **declined** payment is self-service — update the card and retry. A **disputed** payment is not — that needs a person. If you are unsure which one you have, contact support and describe the charge; do not retry a payment you intend to dispute.
