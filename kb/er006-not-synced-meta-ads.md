# ER006 — Not synced with Meta Ads

Polaris shows ER006 when your Meta Ads connection stops returning data, so Facebook and Instagram spend and results stay frozen at their last synced values.

## What ER006 means for your Meta Ads numbers

ER006 affects only the Meta Ads connector. The most common causes are an expired login, a change of Business Manager permissions, or an ad account that moved between Business Managers.

While ER006 is open, Meta Ads widgets repeat the same totals, and any alert watching Meta spend stays quiet because no new data arrives.

## How to fix ER006 in Meta Ads

1. Open **Settings > Connectors** and select the **Meta Ads** connector.
2. Click **Reconnect** and sign in with the Facebook account that manages the ad account. Only an **Admin** can reconnect.
3. When Meta asks which assets to share, make sure the ad account you report on is selected — a reconnect that omits it leaves ER006 open.
4. In Meta Business Manager, confirm the person who reconnected still has access to that ad account, and that the account has no billing hold.
5. Wait for the next scheduled sync and check that spend has moved.

Meta Ads is included on every plan, so ER006 is not a plan limit. If it persists after a reconnect where the ad account was explicitly shared, contact support with the Meta ad account ID.
