# PF003 — Connector not available on your plan

Polaris shows PF003 when you try to add a connector that your current plan does not include. The integration exists and works — your plan simply does not cover it yet.

## What PF003 means, and which connectors it applies to

PF003 is a plan restriction, not an authorization failure. You will see it instead of the **Connect** button on the connector card.

Which connectors each plan includes:

- **Every plan** — Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Slack
- **Growth and above** — HubSpot (CRM) and Mailchimp (email marketing), in addition to the Starter set
- **Enterprise** — Salesforce, in addition to everything on Growth

So PF003 on HubSpot or Mailchimp means you are on Starter, and PF003 on Salesforce means you are on Starter or Growth.

PF003 is not the same as PL001 (you hit the number of connections your plan allows) or PL002 (an upgrade is required to complete an action already in progress).

## How to resolve PF003

1. Open **Settings > Billing** to see which plan your workspace is on.
2. Compare it with the list above to confirm which plan includes the connector you need.
3. Ask an **Admin** to change the plan — only Admins can. Plan changes take effect immediately.
4. Return to **Settings > Connectors** and add the connector; the **Connect** button replaces the PF003 message.

If your plan already includes the connector according to **Settings > Billing** and PF003 still blocks it, contact support with your workspace name and the connector you are trying to add.
