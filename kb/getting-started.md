# Getting started with Polaris

Welcome to Polaris. This guide walks you through the essential first steps to unify your marketing and revenue data.

## 1. Create your workspace

Your workspace is your isolated tenant in Polaris. Only you and invited team members can access it. Sign up at Polaris and name your workspace (e.g., "Acme Marketing").

## 2. Invite your team

Go to **Users & Workspace** → **Invite**. Assign roles:

- **Admin**: can connect data sources and manage team members
- **Analyst**: can build dashboards, reports, and alerts
- **Viewer**: read-only access to dashboards and reports

Start with one Admin (yourself) and invite teammates as needed.

## 3. Connect your first data source

Click **Connectors** and choose from:
- Google Ads
- Google Analytics 4
- HubSpot or Salesforce (CRM)
- Mailchimp or Brevo (email)

Select **OAuth** to authenticate. Polaris will sync your data on a schedule (hourly or daily, depending on your plan).

## 4. Build your first dashboard

Go to **Dashboards** → **Create**. Select metrics from your connected sources—for example, ad spend from Google Ads and leads from your CRM. Dashboards update automatically as new data syncs in.

## 5. Set up your north-star metric

Define the single metric that matters most to your business in **North-Star Metric Tracker**. Polaris will track it across all your data sources and alert you to shifts.
