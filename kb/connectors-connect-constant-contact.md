# How to connect your Constant Contact account

Polaris syncs your Constant Contact campaigns, contacts, and email performance. Constant Contact is a **paid add-on** available on any plan.

## Before you start

- You must have **Admin** role in your Polaris workspace to connect or reconnect integrations.
- The **Constant Contact add-on** must be active on your workspace. Enable it under **Settings > Billing > Add-ons**.
- You'll need Constant Contact login credentials with **Account Owner** or **Manager** access.

## Steps

1. **Open Connectors**
   From your workspace, go to **Settings > Connectors**.

2. **Find Constant Contact**
   Scroll to **Email marketing** and click **Constant Contact**. If you don't see it, activate the add-on first.

3. **Authorize Polaris**
   Click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to Constant Contact's OAuth login.

4. **Sign in to Constant Contact**
   Enter your credentials.

5. **Approve the permissions**
   Review the permissions Polaris requests (read access to campaigns, contacts, and reporting). Click **Allow Access**.

6. **Confirm setup**
   Your Constant Contact is now connected. Polaris will begin syncing on the next scheduled sync (typically within 1 hour).

## What happens next

Email campaign performance (opens, clicks, unsubscribes), contact lists, and engagement events appear in your dashboards. Syncs run every 3 hours.

## Troubleshooting

**Connection failed?**
Ensure your Constant Contact account is active and not in trial expiration status. Also confirm you signed in with a user that has account-level access.

**Data not appearing?**
Check sync status under **Connectors > Sync status**. Constant Contact's initial backfill can take up to 4 hours for accounts with long history.

**"Add-on not active" error?**
Go to **Settings > Billing > Add-ons** and enable the Constant Contact add-on, or contact our team.
