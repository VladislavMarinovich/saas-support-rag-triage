# How to connect your Mailchimp account

Polaris syncs your Mailchimp email campaigns, audiences, and subscriber events. Mailchimp is available on **all plans** (Starter, Growth, and Enterprise) at no extra cost.

## Before you start

- You must have **Admin** role in your Polaris workspace to connect or reconnect integrations.
- You'll need Mailchimp login credentials with **Owner** or **Admin** access to the account.

## Steps

1. **Open Connectors**
   From your workspace, go to **Settings > Connectors**.

2. **Find Mailchimp**
   Scroll to **Email marketing** and click **Mailchimp**.

3. **Authorize Polaris**
   Click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to Mailchimp's OAuth login.

4. **Sign in to Mailchimp**
   Enter your credentials.

5. **Approve the permissions**
   Review the permissions Polaris requests (read access to campaigns, audiences, and reports). Click **Allow**.

6. **Confirm setup**
   Your Mailchimp account is now connected. Polaris will begin syncing on the next scheduled sync (typically within 1 hour).

## What happens next

Campaign performance (opens, clicks, unsubscribes), audience data, and automation stats appear in your dashboards. Available for attribution alongside your other channels. Syncs run every 2 hours.

## Troubleshooting

**Connection failed?**
Ensure the Mailchimp user has Owner or Admin access. If your account uses 2FA, complete the additional prompt during OAuth.

**Data not appearing?**
Check sync status under **Connectors > Sync status**. Mailchimp's initial sync can take up to 3 hours for accounts with many campaigns.

**"Reauthorize required" notification?**
Mailchimp OAuth tokens expire if not used for 90 days. Reconnect through the same steps above.
