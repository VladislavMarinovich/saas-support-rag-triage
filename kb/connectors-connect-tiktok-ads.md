# How to connect your TikTok Ads account

Polaris syncs your TikTok Ads campaigns, ad groups, and cost data. TikTok Ads is a **paid add-on** available on any plan.

## Before you start

- You must have **Admin** role in your Polaris workspace to connect or reconnect integrations.
- The **TikTok Ads add-on** must be active on your workspace. Enable it under **Settings > Billing > Add-ons** or contact support.
- You'll need TikTok Business login credentials with **Admin** or **Standard** access to the ad account.

## Steps

1. **Open Connectors**
   From your workspace, go to **Settings > Connectors**.

2. **Find TikTok Ads**
   Scroll to **Ad platforms** and click **TikTok Ads**. If you don't see it, activate the add-on first.

3. **Authorize Polaris**
   Click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to TikTok Business login.

4. **Sign in to TikTok Business**
   Enter your credentials. Choose the correct Business Center if you manage multiple.

5. **Approve the permissions**
   Review the permissions Polaris requests (read access to ad accounts, campaigns, and reporting). Click **Authorize**.

6. **Select ad accounts**
   Choose the ad accounts to sync.

7. **Confirm setup**
   Your TikTok Ads is now connected. Polaris will begin syncing on the next scheduled sync (typically within 1 hour).

## What happens next

Cost, impressions, clicks, and campaign data appear in your dashboards, available for cross-channel attribution alongside your analytics. Syncs run every 3 hours.

## Troubleshooting

**Connection failed?**
Ensure you're signed in with a TikTok Business account (not a personal TikTok). If you have multiple Business Centers, confirm you're in the correct one.

**Data not appearing?**
Check that the ad account is active and has spend in the last 30 days. Also check sync status under **Connectors > Sync status**. Contact support if the sync completed but no data appears.

**"Add-on not active" error?**
Go to **Settings > Billing > Add-ons** and enable the TikTok Ads add-on, or contact our team.
