# Scheduling and exporting reports

Polaris lets you schedule automated reports and export data on demand. Reports pull your latest synced data from connected sources (Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Brevo, and more).

## Schedule a report

1. Open **Reports** from the left sidebar.
2. Click **New Report** and select your dashboard or metric.
3. Choose your export format (CSV, PDF, or email-ready HTML).
4. Set the delivery schedule:
   - **Frequency:** daily, weekly, or monthly
   - **Day/time:** select preferred delivery window
   - **Recipients:** enter email addresses (comma-separated)
5. Click **Save & Schedule**.

The report will send automatically at your chosen time, pulling fresh data from your last completed sync.

## Export data on demand

1. Open the dashboard or metric you want to export.
2. Click the **Export** button (top right).
3. Choose format (CSV, PDF, or Excel).
4. Click **Download** or **Send to email**.

## Notes

- **Permissions:** only **Admin** and **Analyst** roles can create or edit scheduled reports.
- **Data freshness:** reports reflect your workspace's last successful sync. Check your connector status if data seems stale.
- **Email delivery:** verify recipient addresses are correct. Reports sent to invalid emails will fail silently — check your schedule history if a report doesn't arrive.
