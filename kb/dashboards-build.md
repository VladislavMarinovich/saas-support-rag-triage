# Building your first dashboard

Dashboards in Polaris let you visualize your marketing and revenue data in one place. Here's how to create one.

## Prerequisites

- You must have **Analyst or Admin** permissions in your workspace.
- At least one connector must be active (Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Salesforce, or an email platform).
- Data must have synced at least once. [Check your connector status](link-to-connectors-page).

## Steps

1. **Go to Dashboards** — Click **Dashboards** in the left sidebar.

2. **Create new** — Click the **+ New Dashboard** button (top right).

3. **Name your dashboard** — Enter a clear name (e.g., "Q1 Campaign Performance") and click **Create**.

4. **Add cards** — Click **+ Add Card** to insert a metric, chart, or table.
   - Select your data source (e.g., GA4, Google Ads).
   - Choose a metric (e.g., sessions, conversions, revenue).
   - (Optional) Add filters or break down by dimension (e.g., by campaign).

5. **Arrange & customize** — Drag cards to reorder them. Click the card menu (⋯) to resize, rename, or delete.

6. **Save** — Your dashboard saves automatically.

## Tips

- Use your **North-Star Metric** tracker alongside dashboards for company-wide goals.
- Dashboards refresh hourly by default, matching your connector sync schedule.
- Share dashboards with your team — all workspace members with Viewer+ access can see them.
