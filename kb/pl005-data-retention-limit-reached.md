# PL005 — Data retention limit reached

Polaris shows PL005 when you ask for a date range older than the history your plan keeps. Recent data is unaffected; only the older end of the range is unavailable.

## What PL005 means when you compare year over year

PL005 is the usual surprise in year-over-year reporting: the chart draws the current period and leaves the comparison period empty. Starter keeps the shortest history, Growth extends it, and Enterprise keeps full history. Your window is shown in **Settings > Billing**.

Data outside the window is not recoverable by upgrading — retention applies from the moment it is in force, so an upgrade widens the window going forward, not backwards.

## How to work around PL005

1. Shorten the date range on the dashboard or report until it fits your window.
2. For the history you still have, export it now from the dashboard **Export** button (CSV, PDF, or Excel) so it survives outside Polaris.
3. If you regularly need longer history, ask an **Admin** to move to a plan with a longer retention window in **Settings > Billing**.
4. Rebuild long-range comparisons from exports for the period before the upgrade.

If a range well inside your plan's window returns PL005, contact support with the dashboard name and the dates you requested.
