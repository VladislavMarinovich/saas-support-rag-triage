# AG002 — Question outside knowledge base

Polaris returns AG002 when the assistant has no knowledge base article covering what you asked, so it declines to answer rather than guessing.

## Why the assistant says it does not know

The assistant answers only from Polaris knowledge base articles, and it tells you when it cannot find one. That is deliberate: a confident answer assembled from unrelated articles is worse than an honest AG002, because you cannot tell it apart from a correct one.

AG002 typically appears for questions about things outside the product (general marketing advice, other vendors' tools), features Polaris does not have, or topics no article covers yet.

AG002 is not the same as AG004, where an article may exist but the request has to be handled by a person.

## What to do after AG002

1. Rephrase using the words that appear in the product — the connector name, the module, or an error code such as ER005 or RP001.
2. Ask one thing at a time; a single question retrieves better than three combined.
3. If your question is about your own account rather than how the product works, contact support directly — the assistant answers documentation questions, not account-specific ones.

The assistant replies in the language you write in, including when it returns AG002. If you asked about a documented feature in plain terms and still got AG002, contact support and quote your question so the gap can be closed.
